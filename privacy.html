Privacy Policy

Shorts Factory · Last updated 25 September 2026

Shorts Factory is a personal tool used only by the channel owner to upload their own gameplay videos to their own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

Permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (YouTube Data API scope youtube.upload). It does not read, collect, or store any other Google account data, and it has no other users.

Storage and sharing

The login token is kept only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to or shared with any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Shorts Factory's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/kwlcode/shorts-factory.
